Before choosing equipment Frequently asked questions about cold-climate heat pumps in Great Falls. Will a heat pump keep my Great Falls home warm when it is below zero? + A cold-climate model may be suitable, but its minimum operating temperature alone does not tell you whether it can heat your whole house. Compare the exact model’s heating output at local winter design conditions with your home’s calculated heat loss. The design should explain any capacity shortfall and how supplemental heat or a furnace will cover it. Ask for that cold-weather plan before choosing equipment, rather than relying on a claim that a unit works at -20°F. Sources: PNNL: cold-climate sizing and selection Can I keep my gas or propane furnace and add a heat pump? + That may be an option if the furnace, indoor coil, blower, and controls are compatible with the proposed heat pump. Ask what equipment stays and how changeover will account for capacity, energy prices, and manufacturer requirements. There is no single outdoor temperature at which every Great Falls system should switch to the furnace. Your electric bill may rise while your fuel bill falls, so compare total heating costs and ask for the assumptions behind any savings claim.
