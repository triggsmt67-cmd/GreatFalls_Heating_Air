ADDRESS DECISION: EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
All four audited profiles publicly display street addresses. This snapshot cannot
isolate the effect of address visibility from proximity, reviews, history, links,
business relevance and other differences.
Google says local results depend mainly on relevance, distance and prominence.
Google requires businesses that do not receive customers at their address to
hide/remove their public profile address. A hybrid location can show its address
when it actually receives customers, has permanent signage, and is staffed by
the business during the stated hours. A rented mailing/virtual office is not an
eligible substitute. Confirm Robert's actual operating setup before choosing.
Sterling Sky's own reported tests found rankings/calls declined when addresses
were hidden and recovered after restoration, with a similar result at another
listing. Small observational tests are evidence of a possible real disadvantage,
not proof every service-area business cannot rank. Article currently displays
Sept 22, 2026; comments reference the test as early as 2023, so the underlying
experiment should not be characterized as newly conducted in 2026.
Publishing an address on the WEBSITE and displaying it on GOOGLE'S PROFILE are
separate decisions. The cited test changed profile visibility; it does not prove
putting a private home address in website/footer/schema fixes Maps rankings.
Recommendation: if a genuinely eligible customer-facing location exists, consider
displaying its accurate address on the profile and website, with owner permission.
Otherwise use a legitimate service-area profile, preserve private-address choices,
and measure performance. Do not promise address visibility has zero ranking effect
or that adding a street address guarantees better rankings.
