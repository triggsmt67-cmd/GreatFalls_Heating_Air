# Homepage direction

Audience and market: Great Falls homeowners needing urgent repair, routine care, or replacement advice.
Primary and secondary actions: Original filled call-for-emergency-repair and rebate buttons in the hero. Retain filled estimate buttons in the header, final CTA, and mobile bar.
Verified trust signals: Retain existing license display and service-area language without adding claims.
Reference lessons: Wide photograph below the headline, crossing dark and light sections. No hero card, contractor cutout, decorative badge, or floating statistics.
Visual thesis: Practical Montana craftsmanship expressed through deliberate Manrope typography, softer navy, warm paper, and restrained orange actions.
Signature design devices: Composed white headline, one wide photo bridging sections, original orange and blue call-to-action buttons.
Page and conversion priorities: Preserve approved copy, section order, progressive form behavior, and sticky mobile actions. Keep the service selector close to the photo.
Key responsive decisions: Desktop copy has a compact supporting column; mobile follows headline, supporting copy, actions, and shallow image. Keep Great Falls together.
Missing owner inputs: Replacement contractor photograph. The existing equipment photograph remains in place for layout review.

## Shared site typography

Manrope is the single font family. Shared CSS tokens define page titles, section titles, reading-section headings, subheadings, body copy, and metadata. Section labels use sentence case, weight 500, and restrained tracking. Reading headings use a smaller scale than promotional section headings. Body copy uses 1.75 line height and a 68ch article measure. Forms use 16px inputs, readable labels, and the existing action colors and wording. Service, About, contact, rebate, privacy, confirmation, and 404 pages follow these roles. Shared action rows retain their spacing outside the homepage.
